# Follow-up review

Review who owes the next action before drafting. Respect a prospect's stated
plan and timing, any commitment in a recent call, and the configured cadence.
Use current CRM and thread evidence rather than inventory counters alone.

Gmail inventory is incremental and read-only. Preserve fetch failures and
missing evidence as uncertainty. Match the exact recipient to the verified CRM
person; a participant name or company-domain guess is insufficient.

Show exact copy and recipients for review. Do not draft in a provider, send,
change cadence, or mark a commitment complete without the corresponding user
authorization. Confirm provider delivery and CRM readback separately after an
authorized send. Radar output alone is never delivery proof.
